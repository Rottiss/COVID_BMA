# COVID_BMA

Bayesian Model Averaging (BMA) analysis of COVID-19 cases, deaths, and case fatality across the 184 municipalities of Ceará, Brazil, during the first wave of the pandemic (data through July 31, 2020). Written for an undergraduate thesis (TCC) in Economics at Universidade Federal do Ceará (UFC).

The companion repository, [PyTCC](https://github.com/Rottiss/PyTCC), turns this project's estimation results into the figures and tables used in the manuscript.

## What this does

- Prepares and validates a 184-municipality dataset (`dadosv6.xlsx`) covering demographic, health-capacity, socioeconomic, and political covariates, plus COVID-19 outcomes.
- Estimates BMA models (R package [`BMS`](https://cran.r-project.org/package=BMS), hyper-*g* UIP prior) for three outcomes — cases, deaths, and case fatality — under a main specification (35 covariates) and an extended one that adds two fiscal variables (37 covariates).
- Runs sensitivity specifications: alternative outcome scales (level, `log1p`), and exclusion of Fortaleza (the state capital and a clear outlier in population and density).
- Runs a spatial diagnostic (global Moran's I, Queen/Rook contiguity, `geobr`/`spdep`) on the model-averaged residuals.
- Formats the technical BMA output into the descriptive statistics, result tables, and comparison figures used in the thesis.

## Reproduction

Open `COVID_BMA.Rproj` in RStudio, clear the environment, and run `covid_bma.R` from the top. The script skips chains that have already completed; set the environment variable `COVID_BMA_REPROCESSAR=1` to force re-estimation of an existing chain.

A reduced test mode (`COVID_BMA_MODO_TESTE=1`) writes to `resultados/teste/` for pipeline verification — its coefficients are not used in the thesis and that folder is excluded from version control.

Descriptive statistics, correlation diagnostics, and outcome maps: `analise_descritiva.R` → `resultados/analise_descritiva/`.

Spatial dependence diagnostic: `diagnostico_espacial.R` → `resultados/diagnostico_espacial/`.

Final result tables and comparison figures: `tabelas_resultados.R` → `resultados/tabelas_finais/`.

## Model specification

- Outcomes: log cases per 100k, log deaths per 100k (0.5-death correction), empirical logit of case fatality (0.5 correction).
- All model variables are standardized in R.
- The aggregate social vulnerability index (IVS) is excluded in favor of its three components entered separately.
- Population, area, and density are kept in level form; the without-Fortaleza sensitivity check assesses their skew's influence on the results.
- Main specification excludes federal COVID-19 transfers and emergency income assistance; the extended specification adds both.
- `hyper = "UIP"` is fixed throughout — no sensitivity to a different *g*-prior is estimated.
- Main and extended models: 2 chains per outcome, 1,000,000 retained draws each, 200,000 burn-in. Sensitivity specifications: 1 chain, 500,000 retained draws, 100,000 burn-in.
- A chain is accepted when its minimum PMP correlation is at least 0.95 and, where more than one chain exists, the maximum between-chain PIP difference does not exceed 0.05.

## Notes

- `resultados/estimacoes/**/*.rds` and `resultados/piloto/**/*.rds` (the raw per-chain MCMC objects, tens of MB each) are excluded from version control — they're regenerated by re-running `covid_bma.R`. The consolidated diagnostics, comparison tables, and logs are kept.
- `resultados/teste/` (test-mode runs) is excluded — see Reproduction above.
